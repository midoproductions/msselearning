Our Professional Music Courses

Whether you're picking up an instrument for the first time or looking to pass professional grade examinations, we have structured courses designed specifically for you.

Classical & Contemporary Piano
Starting at GHS 300 / month

Comprehensive keyboard lessons starting from basic posture, finger positioning, reading staff notation, and advancing to baroque, classical, romantic, jazz, and gospel techniques.

  • Learn to read sheet music and play by ear
  • Preparation for international ABRSM grades
  • Private one-on-one sessions in acoustic piano rooms
Enroll in Piano Course
Acoustic & Electric Guitar
Starting at GHS 250 / month

Learn chords, fretboard configurations, finger-picking, strumming structures, and dynamic solos across pop, highlife, classical, blues, and rock genres.

  • Comprehensive scales, arpeggios, and music theory
  • Rhythm guitar accompaniment and soloing tactics
  • Electric, acoustic, and bass guitar specializations
Enroll in Guitar Course
Voice & Vocal Artistry
Starting at GHS 220 / month

Unlock the true potential of your singing voice. We focus on breathing coordination, vocal pitch correction, resonance control, tone, range extension, and emotional expression.

  • Diaphragmatic breath management & vocal safety
  • Solo vocal rehearsals with live accompaniment
  • Performance training for recording and live stage
Enroll in Vocal Course
Percussions & Drum Kit
Starting at GHS 250 / month

Develop exceptional tempo consistency, limb coordination, beat subdivisions, syncopation, and drum fills across a wide array of musical styles.

  • Understanding drum notations & stick control rudiments
  • Drum-set grooves in Highlife, Afrobeat, Reggae, Jazz & Pop
  • Metronome-aligned rhythm and band coordination
Enroll in Drum Course
Violin & Bowed Strings
Starting at GHS 300 / month

Master violin postures, precise bowing strokes, intonations, shifting positions, and rich vibratos for classical soloing or orchestral assemblies.

  • Proper bow hold techniques and tone generation
  • Intonational exercises and finger placement
  • Classical concertos and ensemble repertoire
Enroll in Violin Course
Comprehensive Music Theory
Starting at GHS 150 / month

Understand the science behind the sounds. Learn chord structures, progressions, key signatures, complex time signatures, counterpoint, and orchestration.

  • Reading and writing sheet music in multiple clefs
  • Ear training, interval recognition, and transcription
  • Preparation for Grades 1-8 written exams

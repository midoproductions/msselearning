About Music Solutions School

A leading light in musical literacy, practical performance, and professional audio consultancy in Accra, Ghana. Started and directed by Mr. Dominic Ansah Asare.

Our Heritage & Visionary Founder

Music Solutions School was established with a singular mission: to make formal, world-class music education accessible to passionate learners across Accra and the wider regions of Ghana. Under the expert leadership of our founder, Mr. Dominic Ansah Asare, we have developed a dynamic institute that merges traditional conservatory pedagogy with contemporary technical solutions.

Whether you are taking your very first steps in understanding music notations or looking to refine your professional production techniques, our curriculum is meticulously structured to accelerate your creative evolution. We host fully equipped learning rooms, standard classical and digital keyboard labs, and professional acoustic string setups.

Our Core Values

Aesthetic Excellence: We push the limits of musical expression, precise technique, and pristine audio quality.

Structured Mentorship: Direct face-to-face instruction led by highly certified tutors and instrumentalists.

Creative Innovation: Integrating modern synthesis, production software, and recording environments.

Integrity & Passion: Fostering a supportive, encouraging, and highly ethical learning community.

Our Mission

To cultivate exceptional musical artistry, deep theoretical comprehension, and professional technical skills through personalized instructions, helping student musicians realize their full creative potential in Accra, Ghana.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the premier music school and production consultancy in West Africa, inspiring a legacy of high-caliber performers, songwriters, and audio engineers.

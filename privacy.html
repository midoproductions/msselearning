Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of vital importance. We outline how Music Solutions School protects, stores, and utilizes data collected on our registration forms.

Data Safeguards & Management

We process standard enrollment particulars (names, contact numbers, emails, level of musical training) to schedule physical classroom sessions, allocate instruments, and facilitate billing structures. No student information is sold or transmitted to third-party entities.

All digital payments and physical contact details are hosted on encrypted servers, keeping your records entirely safe under Ghanaian digital information protection policies.

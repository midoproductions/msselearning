Our Professional Music Services

Beyond our structured learning classrooms, Music Solutions School provides high-grade technical services, production packages, studio spaces, and specialized consultation across Accra, Ghana.

Professional Recording Studio Session

Track, mix, and master your songs with highly responsive equipment, premium condenser microphones, and acoustically isolated control rooms.

  • Vocal tracking, instrumental overdubs, and full band setup
  • Mixing and stereo mastering by experienced engineers
  • Sound effects, podcast production, and radio jingle options
Service Details
Acoustic Piano & Instrument Care

Give your precious instruments the delicate handling they need. We perform regular acoustic piano tuning, regulation, and hardware string replacement.

  • Premium piano tuning aligning to A440 Hz standards
  • Guitar action setting, nut filings, and electronic setups
  • Woodwind and brass pad cleaning and replacement
Service Details
Institutional Music Consulting

Our founder Mr. Dominic Ansah Asare leads educational consultancy for colleges, secondary institutions, worship centers, and band directors.

  • Custom music curriculum framework designs
  • Audio equipment list configurations and installation setup
  • Rehearsal dynamics coaching and choir training sessions
Service Details
Live Band Bookings & Event Entertainment

Elevate your weddings, executive events, anniversaries, or concerts with our highly refined corporate live bands playing highlife, jazz, and soul.

  • Highly structured setlists tailored to your event theme
  • Professional PA system, lighting, and sound technicians
  • Energetic vocalists and top-tier instrumentalists

Do I need to own my own musical instrument to enroll?

While we strongly recommend having an instrument at home for your daily practicing sessions, we provide premium-quality keyboards, guitars, violins, and drum kits at our physical school labs during standard class hours.

What is the duration of each music course?

Our standard courses are structured into semesters of 12 weeks. However, we also offer flexible schedules, specialized masterclasses, weekend fast-tracks, and continuous multi-year exam preparation modules.

Are lessons private (one-on-one) or in groups?

We offer both options! Private (one-on-one) lessons are highly customized and recommended for instrument mastery. Group classes are wonderful for theory comprehension, choral training, and learning ensemble playing.

How do I book recording studio sessions?

You can book mixing, mastering, or tracking hours directly through our contact page, by visiting the school premises in Accra, or by sending a message directly on WhatsApp.
